Our Mission

To make the world’s money move as freely as its people

The East Africa–GCC corridor is home to 23 million people who send money across borders every month. Yet the average fee is still 7–10%. That’s $1.8 billion in fees drained from families and small businesses every year.

SOKOPAY exists to end that. We use modern infrastructure, smart FX routing, and mobile money rails to move money faster, cheaper, and with complete transparency.

Milestones

How we got here

2021
Founded in UAE
Three engineers left large banks to solve the remittance problem they saw hurting migrant families every day.
2022
First $1M transferred
Launched beta with M-Pesa integration. First $1M moved in 47 days. 4.9★ App Store rating from day one.
2023
GCC corridor opens
Added UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar rails. Regulated by the Central Bank of Kenya and UAE CBUAE.
2024
Series A — $18M raised
Led by Partech Africa and STV Partners. Expanded to 50+ countries and launched Business accounts.
2025
$2B processed
Crossed $2B in annualised transfer volume. Launched API platform. 1M+ active users across 12 countries.
